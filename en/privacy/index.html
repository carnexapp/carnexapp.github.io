Carnex – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Carnex is an app for keeping a record of your car's services, odometer, renewal dates and fuel purchases. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: Carnex doesn't collect any data. No subscription · no sign-up · your data stays on your phone.

Where is the data stored?

Everything you enter (vehicles, odometer readings, service plans and records, renewal dates, fuel purchases, receipt and document photos, settings) is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Carnex has no server; there is no sign-up and no sign-in.

Data collected and shared

Carnex does not collect any personal data and does not send any to the developer or to third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders for your service plans and renewal dates. Notifications are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent over the internet. Carnex also uses the start-up permission to set reminders up again after the phone restarts.
  • Camera: used only when you take a receipt or document photo for an entry. Photos you pick from your gallery come through Android's own picker; Carnex does not access your whole gallery. Photos stay in the app's own folder.
  • Internet and Google Play billing: only for buying the full version and asking Google Play whether the purchase is valid.

Carnex does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or general access to your phone's files.

Buying the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; your card and payment details stay with Google and Carnex never sees them. Carnex asks Google Play on the phone whether the purchase is valid. Under its own terms, Google may show the developer order details of the sale (e.g. order number, amount, country); these are used only for refunds and bookkeeping.

Backup, export and sharing

When you create a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV) in Settings › Backup, or a service history PDF (full version) from the vehicle menu, the file is prepared on your phone and you choose where to send it (e.g. Google Drive, email, a message). Carnex never sends these files anywhere by itself.

If you turn on the weekly automatic backup, Carnex saves a backup file and copies of new photos once a week to a folder on your phone that you choose. If that folder syncs with a cloud service, the files reach it through that service; Carnex itself never sends them anywhere.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may back up app data to your Google account; the developer has no access to it.

"Rate Carnex" opens Carnex's page on Google Play, and "Tell a friend" opens your phone's share sheet. Your rating and review are subject to Google's terms.

If you write to us

If you use "Send feedback" or email us directly, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and do not share them with anyone.

Deleting data

You can delete entries, vehicles and photos in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all Carnex data from the phone. Backup files you created stay where you put them; you delete those yourself.

Children

Carnex is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children (it collects no data from anyone).

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page.
