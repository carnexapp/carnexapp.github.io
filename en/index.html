  3. 1Plans

    How much is left, at a glance.

    You set each service interval from your car's manual, for example "10,000 km or 12 months". Carnex counts whichever comes first and lets you know when it's close.

  4. 2Renewal calendar

    Your inspection and insurance calendar.

    You enter your inspection, liability insurance, comprehensive and road tax dates; Carnex reminds you when they're close.

  5. 3History

    Your car's logbook.

    Log every service with date, mileage and cost, and add fuel-ups too. Your whole history in one list, whenever you need it, with your service and renewal spending year by year.

  6. 4Backup

    Your backup, in your hands.

    An automatic backup is made every week. Move it wherever you like and restore it on a new phone.

Your data is never locked: in the free version too, you can see, edit, delete and export every entry.

Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Carnex has no server. You don't sign up or log in; there are no ads and no analytics. You back up wherever you choose (phone, SD card or your own cloud folder) and restore on a new phone. Your data only goes where you send it.

Privacy policy

Does the app look up my inspection and insurance dates?

No. You enter the dates; Carnex only reminds you and keeps the record.

Does it need the internet?

No. Carnex runs entirely on your phone.

Is it on iPhone?

For now, Android only; it will be published on Google Play.
